> **The short answer**
> Spend weeks 1 and 2 on setup, weeks 3 and 4 on two or three quick wins, month 2 on your first real tactic, and month 3 on measuring and reviewing. Put the first quarterly review on the calendar today.

> **Three takeaways**
> - **Start before the plan is perfect.** Consistent action, measured and adjusted, beats the best plan on a shelf.
> - **Quick wins earn support.** Tell the team and your leadership what changed and what it did.
> - **A review date turns a plan into a commitment.** Put it on the calendar with everyone who owns part of the plan.

You've done the thinking. You've looked at your skills, mapped your buyer's journey, ranked your ideas and put them in order. Now comes the part no book can do for you: starting.

In the book, the first 90 days are the Foundation stage. The goal isn't to do everything. It's to build momentum, prove the plan works and set up the habits that carry you through the next five stages.

## Why start with 90 days?

**Because a transformation runs in stages, and the first one sets the habits.** In the second edition, Loren and I lay the work out in six stages over six quarters: Foundation, Deployment, Traction, Acceleration, Expansion and Ongoing. You review it every quarter.

The first 90 days are Foundation. Here's how they break down.

## What happens in weeks 1 and 2?

**You set up.** This is the unglamorous part, and it matters most.

- Share the one-page plan with the team.
- Name an owner for each discipline and each priority.
- Agree on your AI ground rules and start your AI usage policy.
- Name a compliance owner and run the compliance checklist, starting with email and texting.
- Check that every lead lands in your CRM with its original source and consent.
- Record your starting numbers, so you have something to compare against.
- Check whether AI assistants mention you for the five to ten buyer questions you track each month.

## What happens in weeks 3 and 4?

**You get two or three quick wins.** Pick them from the top of your ranked list and your effort versus impact grid. Think: finish your Google Business Profile, fix broken forms, add a clear call to action, make your tracking work.

Then celebrate them. Tell the team, and tell your leadership, what changed and what it did. Early proof builds support for the harder work that follows.

## What happens in month 2?

**You launch your first transformational tactic.** Just one. That's your first real change to how the business gets customers.

Two habits help here:

- **Start a shared prompt library** with the AI prompts your team uses most.
- **Hold a 30-minute weekly check-in.** What's done, what's next, what's stuck.

## What happens in month 3?

**You measure and review.** Compare your numbers to your starting point. Then hold your first quarterly business review and ask three questions: What's working? What isn't? What do we change?

Retake the AI fluency self-assessment and the organizational skills assessment too. You'll see what moved.

## What are the six things to keep in front of you?

**Six habits keep the work going.**

1. **Start with your strategy.** Your one-page strategy is the filter for every decision: what you'll do, and what you won't.
2. **Build your team.** Train the people you have, hire for skills you can't build, automate routine work and outsource what you can't do well. Every discipline needs an owner.
3. **Let the data decide.** Track what matters, and use the numbers to decide what to do next and what to stop.
4. **Make room to experiment.** Hold a small share of your effort for new ideas. Expect some tests to fail. That's how you find the ones that work.
5. **Stay connected with your audience.** Publish regularly where your buyers are, answer their questions and ask for reviews.
6. **Climb the AI fluency ladder.** Aim to move up one level by your first quarterly review, and reach Level 4 by the fourth quarter.

## Your next three actions

1. Put your first quarterly review on the calendar today, with everyone who owns part of the plan.
2. Name an owner for each priority, and write your AI ground rules this week.
3. Choose two quick wins you can finish in the next two weeks.

This 90-day sequence is the final module of the three-day Business Transformation workshop, where your team builds its own plan in the room.
