# Content rules

These rules apply to every blog post and article on this site. Follow them when writing or editing content.

- **Wild West Custom Pools:** anywhere a post mentions Wild West Custom Pools by name, link the name to https://www.wildwestcustompools.com. When a post talks about Dan's pool company without naming it, name it on first mention and link it. (Rule set by Mike Huber, Nov 11, 2026.)
- **Post format (approved by Mike, Nov 11, 2026):** every post follows the "Skim, skip, read" template (short answer, three takeaways, jump links, question headings with bold lead-ins, a visual every ~400 words, one real story, a Try-this box with a book prompt and a free tool, FAQ, Your next three actions, invitation, sources). See the doc "Skim, Skip, Read: One Post Format for All Three Sites".
- **Format markup (mikehuber.com, lib/postFormat.ts):** `> **The short answer**`, `> **Three takeaways**`, `> **Why it matters**` and `> **Try this: ...**` blockquotes become boxes; `<!-- toc -->` becomes jump links; an image followed by an *italic line* becomes a captioned figure.
- **Pull quotes and graphics (Nov 11, 2026):** a plain blockquote is a pull quote: `> “line”`, a blank `>` line, then `> — Name`. Graphics live in public/images/blog/<slug>-<n>.png. Orange means AI only. Quotes must be real lines from the post, the book or the person named.
